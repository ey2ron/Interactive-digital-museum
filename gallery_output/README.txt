MINIMALIST GALLERY

Open Minimalist_Gallery.blend. Textures are packed into the model.

Layout: nine wall segments traced directly from the supplied image. Open left and lower-left edges are intentionally preserved. No extra enclosing walls or door openings were invented.
Assumed scale: 0.025 m per reference pixel, overall width 15.6 m, wall height 3.2 m, wall thickness 0.18 m. Change these if actual dimensions are available.

Eight empty display plinths; warm gallery spotlights in recessed linear tracks; seamless concrete floor; matte plaster walls. Two original 8192 x 8192 surface maps drive physically based albedo variation, roughness and bump. These are generated detail maps, not scanned texture sets.

Renderer: Blender Cycles (Octane is not installed).
Cameras: Interior, axonometric layout, and top plan.
Ceiling is hidden in the working viewport to make editing easier; it remains enabled for interior rendering.

Gallery_Interior.png and Gallery_Layout.png are rendered previews.
