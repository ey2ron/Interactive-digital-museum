WANEELLA / AFTER HOURS

Open Waneella_Gallery.blend in Blender. All eight artwork images and both original 8K material maps are packed into the file.

THE MODEL
The existing expanded floor plan, wall geometry, floor geometry, plinth locations and plinth dimensions are preserved. Blue-gray plaster, slate display plinths, subdued concrete, charcoal picture frames, cool ambient illumination, warm gallery lights and restrained amber/rose accents establish the theme. High-level conduits, two small plants and a retro monitor add urban detail without adding floor obstacles.

The original Minimalist_Gallery.blend has not been modified. The separate unsaved scene already open in Blender was not modified or replaced.

ARTWORK
Eight actual Waneella works are shown as static first frames from the artist's public animated images. No cropping, stretching or generated replacements. Image textures use Closest interpolation to retain pixel edges. Each frame has a title and artist credit; full source URLs are embedded in the ARTWORK_SOURCES text block and in Waneella_Artwork_Sources.txt.

The original east-partition display had a missing parent and detached components. Its mount has been restored to the intended position from the original placement script, with the existing 25% horizontal plan expansion applied.

The model opens with the entrance camera selected for rendering. The ceiling is hidden only in the editing viewport; it remains present in interior renders. The layout render temporarily hides ceiling equipment and adds a broad softbox; these overview-only changes are not saved into the interior scene.

Renderer: Cycles. The model is saved at 1500 x 1000 with 48 samples and denoising; the delivered views use 64 samples and denoising.
