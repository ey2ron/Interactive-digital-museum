# Artwork descriptions

Descriptions and image alternatives live separately in `public/assets/artwork-notes.json` so a Blender export does not overwrite editorial copy. All sixteen works have visual alt text written for this gallery.

Research checked on 12 November 2026:

- Waneella's [official website](https://waneella.com/) links to the publisher's [Shimmer page](https://www.harugonomayu.com/shop/p/shimmer), which includes a descriptive note. The gallery paraphrases it and labels the text **Adapted from Harugo No Mayu**. The page does not identify the note's author, so it is not presented as a quotation from Waneella.
- Waneella's [Second Chance post](https://waneella.tumblr.com/post/754537868030001152/second-chance-new-illustration-for-this-years) links to the [publisher's artwork page](https://www.harugonomayu.com/shop/p/second-chance-by-waneella). Its note about caring for a sprout is paraphrased with the same publisher attribution.
- The accessible original Tumblr posts for Let the Moon Shine, Milk Tea, Night Sky, Still Night and Purple Fatigue contain titles and promotional/social links, rather than interpretive descriptions.
- The remaining original posts were attempted, but could not be retrieved by the research tools. This is a research limitation, not evidence that no artist description exists elsewhere.

For the other fourteen works, the gallery uses original descriptions based on the locally supplied images, labelled **Gallery description**. These describe visible details and offer gentle interpretations; they are not claims about the artist's intended meaning. Original artwork links remain available for every work. No third-party text is reproduced in full, and publisher descriptions are paraphrased rather than quoted.

When an artist-authored description becomes available, replace the corresponding description, update its credit and source, and record the evidence here.
