ABOUT THE ARTIST

Waneella

Small pixels. Expansive worlds.

Waneella is the pixel artist Valeriya Sanchilo. With a background in animation, she creates intricate urban scenes that bring the visual language of classic video games into richly detailed, atmospheric worlds.

Her work moves between illustrations, prints and ambient animations, with music by e.sanchillo. Her books Decade and Pixelscapes offer a closer look at her artwork and creative process.
